## Overview
Driven by global sustainability trends, paper‑based packaging is widely adopted to replace single‑use plastic. However, plain paper / molded‑pulp substrates lack water, oil and gas‑blocking performance.

Humanchem’s water‑based paper barrier coatings are designed to **replace PE film lamination and fluorinated‑PFAS barrier materials**. While adding water‑proof, grease‑proof and gas‑barrier capability, the coatings retain paper’s original recyclability, repulpability and printability for paper converters and pulp‑molded tableware manufacturers.

> This repo stores public technical reference materials, not production source‑code.

## Two Core Product Lines
### 1. General Paper Packaging Barrier Coatings
Target for paper converters, paper cups, cartons and take‑away paper packaging.
Deliver moisture / grease barrier while preserving good printability and converting performance.

Typical end‑uses:
- Paper cups & paper food‑service packaging
- Bakery & snack cartons
- Take‑away paper containers
- Specialty functional paper packaging

### 2. Pulp‑Molded Food‑Container Barrier Coatings
Dedicated for molded‑fiber tableware. Solve pain‑points of uncoated pulp: poor hot‑water / hot‑oil resistance for food‑service scenarios.
Fluorine‑free & food‑contact compliant; keep molded‑pulp repulpable and compostable.

Typical end‑uses:
- Molded‑pulp trays, bowls, plates, clamshell take‑away boxes
- Disposable molded‑fiber food‑service packaging

## Key Technical Features
- ✅ **Multi‑dimensional barrier**: block water‑vapor, oxygen, CO₂ and grease / fat, protect inner‑content quality
- ✅ **Sustainable water‑borne formulation**: non‑toxic, solvent‑free options; PFAS / fluorine‑free; comply with global food‑contact standards such as GB4806, GB9685
- ✅ **Preserved printability**: compatible with flexographic, gravure, offset lithographic & digital printing processes after coating
- ✅ **Processing flexibility**: adapt to diverse paper grades / thicknesses, fit for both **in‑line coating** and **off‑line coating** workflows
- ✅ Additional properties: anti‑blocking, reliable heat‑sealing performance, enhanced fiber‑substrate bonding strength

## Lab Performance Test Cases
> All results are achieved under defined laboratory conditions. Real‑world performance varies with substrate, coating weight and drying parameters.

1. **Water‑proof for paper cup**: Coated paper cup holds 100 °C hot water / coffee / tea, no leakage for 24 hours
2. **Oil‑resistance for pulp‑molded container**: Pour hot oil directly; container stays non‑leaked for 30 minutes
3. **Anti‑blocking test**: Fold coated paper, press under 5 kg weight, bake 60 °C × 2 h. Cool down; coated surfaces can separate easily without sticking together
4. **Heat‑sealing property**: Tear heat‑sealed sample; tearing occurs on paper fiber layer, no peeling‑off of coating layer

> For pulp‑molded dedicated grades: resist 100 °C boiling‑water & 100 °C hot‑oil soaking for 30 min; Cobb value ≤ 3 under specified test settings.
